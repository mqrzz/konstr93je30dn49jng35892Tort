Dedicated IP

A sending address of your own

Your reputation stays separate and under your control. Available on request on the Business plan, and included with Enterprise.

When it helps

A good fit, and not yet

A good fit if

  • You send steadily and in volume
  • You want your reputation separate from others

Not needed yet if

  • You are starting to send
  • Your volume is small or irregular
How to get one

From request to sending

  1. 01

    Send a request

    Use the request button on the pricing page or write to support from your account.

  2. 02

    We prepare the address

    The address is assigned to your account and you receive the records to publish.

  3. 03

    Increase volume gradually

    A new address has no history, so start small and grow over a few weeks.
