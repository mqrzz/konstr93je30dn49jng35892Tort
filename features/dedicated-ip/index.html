Choosing

When a dedicated IP makes sense

Most senders are better on shared addresses. Pick the one that matches your volume.

A dedicated IP is a good fit when

  • You send steadily and in volume
  • You want your reputation separate from others
  • Your mail is expected at predictable times

A shared IP is the better choice when

  • You are starting to send
  • Your volume is small or irregular
  • You prefer no warm-up work
