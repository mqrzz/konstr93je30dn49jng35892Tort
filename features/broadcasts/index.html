Broadcasts

One message, every subscriber

Write the email once, choose an audience and send it now or later. Unsubscribe links, suppression and results are handled for you.

How it goes

From draft to results

  1. 01

    Write in the editor

    Use blocks or paste HTML. Variables such as the contact’s name are filled in for each recipient.

  2. 02

    Choose who gets it

    Pick an audience. Contacts who unsubscribed, bounced or complained are skipped automatically.

  3. 03

    Send and watch progress

    A live progress bar shows how many messages are processed while the queue works through the list.

  4. 04

    Read the results

    Delivered, bounced, complaints and unsubscribes are counted for every broadcast.

Built for trust

What is handled for you

Every message

  • A one-click unsubscribe link and the matching header
  • Your own DKIM signature

Every list

  • Unsubscribed contacts never receive another broadcast
  • Bounced and complaining addresses are skipped

Questions about broadcasts

Can I send to contacts I imported?

Only to people who agreed to hear from you. The acceptable use policy requires consent, and complaints affect your account rating.

Where do unsubscribes go?

To a public unsubscribe page. The contact is marked in your audience right away.
