Questions

Which DNS record do I need?

One MX record on your domain with priority 10, pointing to the host shown in Domains. Geserd checks it and marks receiving as ready.

What happens above the limit?

New messages are refused until the next month or until you move to a larger plan.

Are attachments stored?

The message record lists each attachment with its name, type and size.
