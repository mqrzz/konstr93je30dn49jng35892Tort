DNS

Exactly the records you need

The dashboard shows the precise DKIM, SPF and MX records for your domain, generated the moment you add it. Copy them into your DNS provider and press verify. There is nothing to guess and nothing generic: every value is specific to your domain and your account.

  • Sending needs DKIM and SPF
  • Receiving needs the MX record
  • DMARC is recommended, not required

  • Re-check at any time after a DNS change

Subdomains

Keep transactional mail separate

We recommend sending from a subdomain such as mail.yourdomain.com instead of your root domain. This keeps the sending reputation of your transactional mail separate from your main domain, so a spike in bounces from one project never affects the other, and you can verify several subdomains independently.

  • Root domain reputation stays protected
  • Verify several subdomains independently
  • Domain limits depend on your plan

Questions about domains

Should I use a subdomain?

We recommend it, for example mail.yourdomain.com. It keeps transactional reputation separate from your main domain.

Do you manage my DNS?

No. You keep full control of your DNS and add the records at your own provider.

How many domains can I add?

It depends on your plan, from one on Free to ten on Business, and unlimited on Enterprise.

Can two accounts verify the same domain?

No. A domain can be verified by one account at a time.
