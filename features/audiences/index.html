Audiences

Lists that look after themselves

Paste addresses or import a CSV file. Geserd remembers who unsubscribed, bounced or complained, so you do not mail them by mistake.

At a glance

What an audience keeps

Email addressOne row per contact. Duplicates are merged when you import.
NameOptional. Available in templates as a variable.
StatusSubscribed, unsubscribed, bounced or complained, updated as events arrive.
Search and filtersFind a contact by address or filter by status.
Working with contacts

Add, import, clean

Paste or import

Paste addresses separated by commas or line breaks, or upload a CSV file with a header row.

Honest unsubscribes

Unsubscribing takes one click and is final. We do not re-add a contact you cannot mail.

Used by broadcasts

Pick an audience when you create a broadcast. Only subscribed contacts are included.

Questions about audiences

How many contacts can I store?

The limit depends on your plan. You see your current usage on the Audiences page and in your profile.
