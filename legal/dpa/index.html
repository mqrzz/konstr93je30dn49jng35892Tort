Data Processing Agreement

How Geserd processes your recipients’ personal data on your behalf, and the security measures we apply.

Effective 2026-10-11

Scope and roles

This agreement applies when you use Geserd to send, receive or store personal data about other people, such as your recipients, subscribers and contacts (“Customer Data”). It is part of the Terms of Service and applies automatically; no separate signature is needed. If this agreement and the Terms conflict about the processing of Customer Data, this agreement applies.

You are the controller of Customer Data (or the equivalent under the law that applies, for example the operator under Russian law Federal Law No. 152-FZ). We are your processor and process Customer Data only on your behalf. If you are yourself a processor for another controller, we are your sub-processor and you promise that your instructions match those of the controller.

In short

We process your recipients’ data only to provide the service you asked for. We keep it secure, tell you about breaches, help you answer people’s requests, and delete it when you leave or when your plan’s retention ends.

Your instructions

We process Customer Data only as described in this agreement, the Terms and your use of the features of the service, and as you instruct us through the dashboard or API. We tell you if we think an instruction breaks the law, and we may pause carrying it out until it is clarified. You are responsible for having a lawful basis for the processing, for telling your recipients how their data is used, and for the content you send.

Confidentiality

We make sure that the people who can access Customer Data are bound by confidentiality, and that access is limited to those who need it to run, secure and support the service. We do not read the content of messages except where needed to operate the service, to investigate abuse or a technical problem, to meet a legal obligation, or with your permission.

Security

We apply technical and organizational measures suited to the risk. The measures at the date of this agreement are listed in Annex 1. We may update them if the new measures are not weaker overall.

Subprocessors

You allow us to use the subprocessors listed on the subprocessors page. We bind each of them to data protection duties that are no less protective than these. We are responsible for their performance of those duties. We tell you before we add or replace a subprocessor that handles the content of Customer Data, as described on that page, and you may object on reasonable grounds.

Requests from data subjects

If a person asks us about data we process for you, we tell them to contact you and, where the law requires, tell you about the request. The dashboard lets you export, correct and delete Customer Data yourself, and manage unsubscribes and suppression. We help you answer requests where the dashboard does not already allow it, within a reasonable time.

Assistance

Taking into account the nature of the processing and the information we have, we help you meet your duties on security, breach notification, impact assessments and consultations with authorities. We may ask a reasonable fee for help that goes beyond the normal features of the service and takes significant effort.

Personal data breaches

If we become aware of a breach of security that leads to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of or access to Customer Data, we tell you without undue delay and at the latest within 48 hours, by email to your account address and in the dashboard. The message describes the nature of the breach, the data and people affected as far as we know, the likely effects and the steps we are taking. We act quickly to limit the harm and keep you informed.

International transfers

You allow us to process Customer Data on our servers and with the subprocessors on the list. Where the GDPR or a similar law requires a transfer mechanism and no other one applies, the parties incorporate the EU standard contractual clauses, Module Two (controller to processor) or Module Three (processor to processor), by reference. Annex 1 and the details of this agreement complete the clauses; the supervisory authority and the governing law for the clauses are those of the country in the EEA where you are established, or, if you are not established there, of the EU member state in which your data subjects are located. Similar arrangements apply to transfers governed by Swiss or UK law.

Return and deletion

You can export your audiences, templates and data through the dashboard and API at any time. When you delete data or close your account, we delete Customer Data within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it, and backups, if any, are overwritten within 35 days. The retention periods for logs, emails and metrics in the Privacy Policy apply while your account is open. On request, we confirm in writing that deletion has been done.

Information and audits

On request, we give you the information needed to show that we meet this agreement, including written answers to reasonable security questionnaires. If that does not satisfy a legal audit requirement that applies to you, you may carry out an audit once a year with 30 days’ notice, during business hours, under confidentiality, at your own cost and without harming other customers or the security of the service.

Liability

Each side’s liability under this agreement is subject to the limits in the Terms of Service, except where the law does not allow the limit. Nothing here limits a data subject’s rights against a controller or a processor.

Term

This agreement lasts as long as we process Customer Data for you. The sections on confidentiality, deletion, liability and anything that by nature should survive stay in force afterwards.

Annex 1: security measures

  • Encryption in transit: TLS for the website, dashboard, API and SMTP submission; opportunistic TLS for mail delivery to other servers where they support it.
  • Secrets: API keys, session tokens and one-time codes are stored only as hashes; private DKIM keys are encrypted at rest; request logs remove secrets and shorten long bodies.
  • Access control: each customer’s data is separated in the application and reachable only through that customer’s authenticated account or keys; keys can be limited and revoked; administrative access is limited to the operator and is used only for support, security and legal reasons.
  • Abuse protection: rate limits, spam checks, suppression of addresses that bounce or complain, unsubscribe on every broadcast, and automated monitoring of delivery quality.
  • Retention: automatic deletion of emails, events and logs at the end of the period of your plan, and of metrics at the end of their period.
  • Resilience: monitoring of the service and errors, controlled updates, and recovery procedures for the servers.
  • Incident response: the breach process above, with the operator as the point of contact.

We review these measures from time to time and improve them as threats change.

Приложение 1: меры безопасности

  • Шифрование при передаче: TLS для сайта, личного кабинета, API и SMTP-отправки; оппортунистический TLS при доставке почты на другие серверы, где они его поддерживают.
  • Секреты: API-ключи, токены сессий и одноразовые коды хранятся только в виде хешей; закрытые ключи DKIM шифруются при хранении; в логах запросов секреты удаляются, а длинные тела сокращаются.
  • Контроль доступа: данные каждого клиента разделены в приложении и доступны только через аутентифицированный аккаунт или ключи этого клиента; ключи можно ограничивать и отзывать; административный доступ есть только у оператора и используется только для поддержки, безопасности и по закону.
  • Защита от злоупотреблений: ограничения частоты, проверки на спам, подавление адресов, которые дали отказ или пожаловались, отписка в каждой рассылке и автоматическое наблюдение за качеством доставки.
  • Хранение: автоматическое удаление писем, событий и логов по окончании срока вашего тарифа и метрик по окончании их срока.
  • Устойчивость: наблюдение за сервисом и ошибками, контролируемые обновления и процедуры восстановления серверов.
  • Реагирование на инциденты: описанный выше порядок уведомления об утечках, контактное лицо — оператор.

Мы время от времени пересматриваем эти меры и улучшаем их по мере изменения угроз.
