Acceptable Use Policy

Sending on shared infrastructure means your habits affect other customers. These rules exist to keep deliverability high for everyone.

Draft. This document has not been reviewed by a lawyer and will be replaced before public launch.

Not allowed

Unsolicited bulk email of any kind, phishing, fraud, malware, illegal content, content that harasses or endangers people, and sending to purchased, rented or scraped address lists.

Required

Send only to recipients who reasonably expect your email, honor unsubscribe and complaint signals promptly, and keep your bounce and complaint rates low. If you are unsure whether a use case fits, ask us before you send.

Enforcement

We may throttle, pause or suspend sending for an account, with or without notice depending on severity, to protect deliverability for everyone on our infrastructure. Repeated or serious violations can lead to account termination.
