Privacy Policy

This describes what we collect to run geserd, why, and the choices you have about it.

Draft. This document has not been reviewed by a lawyer and will be replaced before public launch.

What we collect

Your account email and name, the domains you verify, and the email you send and receive through geserd, including headers and the content needed to deliver and display it in your dashboard. We also keep request logs for security and debugging.

How we use it

To operate the service: deliver your mail, show it in your dashboard, calculate your usage against plan limits, and detect abuse that could damage deliverability. We do not sell your data and we do not read message content for advertising.

Retention

Message data and logs are kept for the retention period of your plan, shown on the pricing page, and then deleted. Account data is kept until you delete your account, at which point it is removed.

Your rights

You can ask us, through the contact page, to export or delete your data at any time. You can also delete a domain or your entire account yourself from the dashboard.
