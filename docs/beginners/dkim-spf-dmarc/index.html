DKIM, SPF and DMARC

Three checks that prove an email really comes from you.

DKIM: the signature

Every message is signed with a private key. Receivers look up the matching public key in your DNS and confirm nothing changed on the way.

SPF: the guest list

SPF lists which servers may send mail for your domain. A message from any other server looks suspicious.

DMARC: the rule

DMARC tells receivers what to do when DKIM or SPF fails, and where to send reports. Start with p=none to watch, then tighten later.
