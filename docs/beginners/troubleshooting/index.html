If an email does not arrive

Most delivery problems have a short list of causes. Go through them in this order before you contact anyone.

1. Look at the log

Open Logs and find the message. The status tells you where it stopped. “Delivered” means the other side accepted it, so look in the spam folder. “Failed” shows the reason in words, and “Bounced” means the address does not exist or the mailbox is full.

2. Check the domain

Open Domains and press verify again. If any record shows as missing, go back to the DNS guide and compare it with the value in Geserd. Records can also disappear when someone changes the domain’s name servers, so check that the DNS page you edited is the one in use.

3. Check the recipient

  • Typos in the address are the most common cause of a bounce.
  • Suppressed addresses. After repeated bounces or a spam complaint Geserd stops sending to an address to protect your reputation. You will see it in the suppression list.

4. Improve the message itself

Short subjects without capital letters and exclamation marks, a clear sender name, a plain-text version next to the design, and an unsubscribe link in newsletters all help. Messages that are mostly an image, or that contain only a link, are often treated as spam.

Still stuck? Open a ticket from the dashboard and include the message from the log. We will tell you what happened and what to change.
