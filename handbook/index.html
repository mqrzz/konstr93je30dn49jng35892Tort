Company

Why we exist

Email is still the most reliable way to reach people, and still painful to set up. We exist to put signing, delivery, logs and inbound parsing behind one API so small teams can ship mail without becoming email experts.

Company

What we believe

One clear way beats five configurable ones. We say what works today and what does not. We keep limits even when they are inconvenient, because deliverability is a promise to every sender.

Company

How we run the service

We operate our own servers instead of reselling another email service. Capacity grows with real usage, and every plan has limits that protect sender reputation.

Engineering

How we ship changes

Every change that you can see or feel is written down in the public changelog with a date, in four languages. If it is not in the changelog, we did not announce it.

Engineering

How we handle incidents

Problems are posted on the status page as they happen, with their impact and current state. We update the entry until it is resolved and keep it visible afterwards.

Engineering

How we protect your data

API keys are shown once and stored only as a hash. Mail travels over TLS and is signed with DKIM. You can export your data or delete your account yourself from the account page.

Support

How we help you

Open a ticket from the dashboard or use the contact page. Replies arrive in the ticket and by email, in the language you picked.

Support

How we use feedback

Bug reports and rough edges are the most useful thing you can send. The feedback card in the dashboard goes straight to us, and fixes land in the changelog.
