What is our philosophy for anti-abuse

Reading time5 min
Updated2026-10-14
AuthorsGGeserd team

Every sender shares the same reputation with the world’s mail servers. One abuser can make email worse for all of them, so we take it seriously.

Why we care

Mail providers judge a sender by what recipients do: how many report a message as spam, how many addresses do not exist, how many bounce. A service that lets bad mail through sees its good mail filtered too. Protecting deliverability is therefore not a favour to the receiving side; it is the thing you are paying us for.

Rules everyone can read

The acceptable use policy says what is not allowed on Geserd: unsolicited bulk mail, purchased lists, deceptive messages, phishing and anything illegal where you or the recipient live. It is written in plain language and lives on its own page, so nobody can say they were not told.

How we notice problems

  • Signals from the mail itself. Bounce rate, complaint rate and unsubscribe rate are tracked for every account and shown to you as a delivery score from 0 to 100, where higher is better.
  • Signals from behaviour. Many accounts created from the same place for the same purpose are a pattern that we look for, because abusers rarely stop at one account.
  • Reports from people. If you receive mail you did not ask for, tell us; every report is read.

What we do about it

We act in steps that match the problem. A first warning gives you the chance to fix a mistake. A temporary freeze stops sending while we talk. A block ends the account when abuse is clear. Automatic checks propose the step, and a person looks at the evidence and decides, so that an honest mistake is not treated like a campaign. You can always ask why through a ticket and receive an actual reason.
