How we use feedback

Reading time3 min
Updated2026-10-14
AuthorsGGeserd team

Bug reports and rough edges are the most useful thing you can send us. They are how the service gets better.

How to send it

Feedback is a topic when you open a ticket, so it reaches the people who read tickets rather than disappearing into a form. Say what you tried, what you expected and what happened. A screenshot or the text of an error is worth a paragraph. You do not need to propose a solution; describing the problem well is already most of the work.

What happens next

  • We reproduce it. If we can see the problem ourselves, it moves from a report to a task.
  • We fix what is small, now. A typo, a clipped label or a wrong colour is repaired the same day.
  • We write down what is large. Bigger requests go into the plan with the reason they matter, and when they ship they appear in the changelog.

We cannot promise to build everything we are asked for. We do promise that a report is read, that you get an honest answer, and that we will tell you when a thing you reported has been fixed.
