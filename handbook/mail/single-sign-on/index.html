Single sign-on for companies

Reading time5 min
Updated2026-10-17
AuthorsGGeserd team

With single sign-on your team opens Geserd with the company account they already use. No new password, and access ends the moment a person leaves the company.

Who it is for

Single sign-on is part of the Enterprise plan and is set up by our team under your contract. It fits companies that already run an identity provider such as Microsoft Entra ID, Google Workspace, Okta, Keycloak or any service that speaks OpenID Connect.

How the sign-in works

  • Your colleague enters a work email on the sign-in page. Geserd recognises the company domain.
  • They are sent to your provider and sign in there, with your password rules and your second factor.
  • Your provider confirms who they are. Geserd checks the signature, the audience and that the email belongs to your domain.
  • They land in the dashboard. The first time, Geserd creates the account; later it recognises it.

What we need from you

Register a new application in your identity provider, with the redirect address we give you, and send us four things: the company email domain, the issuer address, the client ID and the client secret. We check the connection before we turn it on. The secret is stored encrypted and is never shown again, not even to our own staff.

Good to know

  • One domain, one provider. Each company domain points to one connection, which we can turn off at any moment.
  • Verified email only. If your provider does not confirm the address, sign-in is refused.
  • Offboarding is yours. Disable the person in your provider and they cannot sign in again.
