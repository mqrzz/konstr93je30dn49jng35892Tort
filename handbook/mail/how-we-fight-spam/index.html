How we fight spam

Reading time5 min
Updated2026-10-17
AuthorsGGeserd team

Everyone who sends through Geserd shares the same standing with the mail servers of the world. That is why we check mail on the way out, watch behaviour, and act early.

A score before every send

Every outgoing message gets a spam score from a set of readable rules: shouting subjects, hidden text, link shorteners, missing text part, suspicious attachments and similar patterns. In the sending settings you choose how strict we are: off, balanced or strict. A rejected message is never sent and does not count against your limits, and the response tells you which rules fired so you can fix the text.

Behaviour, not only content

Spammers rarely stop at one account. We look at patterns around accounts: many registrations from one place in a short time, the same browser behind several accounts, the same tax number in several applications, disposable email domains. These raise a flag. A flag is not a verdict: a person reviews the evidence before anything permanent happens, so an honest mistake is never treated like a campaign.

Three levels of response

  • A warning. You hear about the problem first and get the chance to fix it.
  • A freeze. Sending stops while we talk, but you can still use the dashboard and write to support.
  • A block. Used only when abuse is clear. You are told why, and you can ask a person to look again.

What you can do to stay clear

Send only to people who expect your mail, honour unsubscribes the same day, keep one account per organisation and keep your delivery score high. The acceptable use policy lists the rules in plain language, and the account rating on your profile shows how Geserd sees your sending right now.
