How we make money

Reading time4 min
Updated2026-10-14
AuthorsGGeserd team

We charge for sending and receiving email. That is the whole business model, and it shapes how we treat you.

Plans, not tricks

There are four plans: Free, Pro, Business and Enterprise. Free lets you try the whole service on one domain with low limits, so you can see that mail really arrives before you pay anything. Pro and Business raise the daily and monthly limits, the number of domains, keys and webhook endpoints, and how long logs are kept. Enterprise is for custom limits and a contract.

Prices are shown in the currency of your region: roubles for visitors from Russia and neighbouring countries, another currency elsewhere, converted by the exchange rate. The number you see is the number you pay.

What we do not sell

  • Your data. It is not a product and never becomes one.
  • Your attention. There are no advertising or analytics cookies on the site.
  • Surprises. If a limit changes, if a price changes, you hear about it before it applies to you.

Why this model is good for you

When revenue comes only from delivered mail, our interests line up with yours. We earn when your messages arrive, we lose when they do not, and the cheapest way to keep earning is to be reliable, plain and fair. We also support companies working in specific regions with a dedicated tariff on its own page, because a rate that suits a large company in one country can be out of reach for a small business in another.
