Conditions

  • The tariff is for business use and is granted to one account per organization.
  • The details in your application must be true. We may ask for supporting documents.
  • The discount applies to Pro and Business. Enterprise is priced individually.
  • Approval is not automatic. We can decline an application or end the tariff if the conditions are not met.
  • Our terms of service and acceptable use policy apply as usual.
Can I use it for a personal project?

No. The tariff is for businesses and services. For personal projects the regular free plan is the right choice.

Does the discount apply to Enterprise?

No, Enterprise is priced individually. Contact us and we will discuss it.

What if my application is rejected?

You will see the reason in your account and can apply again with corrected details.

Which documents do you need?

Usually only the tax ID you enter in the form. If something is unclear we write to you and ask for a registry extract or a self-employed certificate.

What happens if I leave Siberia?

Tell us. The discount and the larger limits end, your data stays, and your limits return to the normal plan values.
