What is GDPR?

The General Data Protection Regulation is the European Union law that governs how personal data is collected, used and protected.

It gives people rights over their data: to be informed, to access it, to correct it, to erase it, to object and to take it elsewhere.

Who audited Geserd?

Nobody yet. Geserd has no external audit or certification. We describe what we do on the compliance page, so you can judge for yourself.

How do I get a signed DPA?

The data processing agreement applies to every account from the moment you sign up. You can read it on the documents page and in the legal section.

Where is my data stored?

The hosting providers and the countries where data is processed are listed on the subprocessors page. We update that list before we change it.

How long do you keep data?

Email logs are kept for the number of days of your plan: one day on Free, seven on Pro, thirty on Business and a year on Enterprise. When you delete your account, your data is deleted too.

How do I exercise my rights?

Write to support from the address on your account. We send a copy of your data, correct it or delete it, and you may object to processing or contact the data protection authority of your country.
