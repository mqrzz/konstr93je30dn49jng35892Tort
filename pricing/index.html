Frequently asked questions

Do I need a credit card for Free?

No. Create an account and start sending on the Free plan without entering any payment details.

Why is the Free plan so small?

Free exists to let you verify a domain and test your integration end to end. Geserd runs on shared infrastructure with a single IP, so we keep free volume small to protect deliverability for everyone. For real sending, Pro is the intended step.

What counts toward my limit?

Every accepted send request counts toward the monthly and daily send limits. Received messages count toward the inbound limit separately.

How are inbound emails counted?

Each message delivered to one of your verified domains and stored counts once, regardless of size or number of recipients. Messages rejected as spam before storage do not count.

What happens if I send more emails than my plan allows?

Without pay-as-you-go, sending returns an error until the period resets or you upgrade, and mail already queued is still delivered. With pay-as-you-go enabled on a paid plan, extra emails are billed at your overage rate.

Can I change plans?

Yes, at any time, up or down. A downgrade takes effect at the start of your next billing period.

What payment methods do you accept?

Billing is being finished during the beta. Cards will be supported first, with local payment methods for customers in Russia and neighbouring countries. Until billing opens, paid plans are not charged.

Is there a free trial?

The Free plan has no time limit, so you can test as long as you like. If you need higher limits for a proof of concept, write to us and we will raise them temporarily.

Who can I contact about a custom plan?

Use the contact page and choose Enterprise. Tell us your expected volume and requirements, and we will reply with a proposal.
