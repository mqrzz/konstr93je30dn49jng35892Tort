Pricing questions

Do I need a credit card for Free?

No. Create an account and start sending on the Free plan without entering any payment details.

What counts toward my limit?

Every accepted send request counts toward the monthly and daily send limits. Received messages count toward the inbound limit separately.

What happens when I reach a limit?

Sending returns an error until the period resets or you upgrade. Nothing is charged automatically, and mail already queued is still delivered.

Can I change plans?

Yes, at any time, up or down. A downgrade takes effect at the start of your next billing period.

Why are the limits low?

geserd runs on shared infrastructure with a single IP during the beta. Limits grow as capacity grows, instead of promising numbers we cannot support yet.
