The plan

Five steps, in this order

  1. 01

    Add your domain to Geserd

    Publish the DNS records from your dashboard next to the old ones. Both providers can sign your mail at the same time.

  2. 02

    Recreate templates and keys

    Rebuild the templates in the editor and create API keys, one per application.

  3. 03

    Switch a small part of traffic

    Send a few messages through Geserd and read the logs before you move the rest.

  4. 04

    Move your suppression list

    Import addresses that bounced, complained or unsubscribed, so nobody is mailed by mistake.

  5. 05

    Switch inbound and webhooks

    Change the MX record last, then point your webhook URL at Geserd events.

What changes in your code

Usually only

  • Field names for sender and recipient

Not needed

  • Changing your domain
  • Rewriting your email content

Questions about moving

Can I use both providers during the move?

Yes. Keep the old records until the logs show everything works, then remove them.
