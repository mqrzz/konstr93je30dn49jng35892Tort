Philosophy

Mail should just arrive

Four principles decide what we build, what we refuse to build and how we talk to you.

Get startedDocumentation
1

Make it arrive.

A message that does not arrive has failed, however nice the dashboard. Signing, queues and logs come before decoration.

2

Friction must be zero.

From the first sign-up to the first email should take minutes. Every extra step is a reason to leave.

3

Documentation is the product.

Nobody should have to ask how something works. We write the guide first and then check that the product matches it.

4

Say what happened.

Every email keeps its history and every error says what to change. We would rather show a failure than hide it.

5

Predictable by design.

The API should not surprise you. Names stay stable, changes are announced and old behavior keeps working.

6

Respect the recipient.

Unsubscribing is one click and final, and consent is required. Complaints affect your account rating.

7

Finished or not shipped.

We add a feature when it works, not when it is announced. No stub pages and no promised features.
