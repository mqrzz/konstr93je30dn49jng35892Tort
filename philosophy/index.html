Principles

What we hold to

  1. 01

    Delivery before decoration

    A message that does not arrive has failed, however nice the dashboard. We spend effort on signing, queues and logs first.

  2. 02

    Say what happened

    Every email keeps its history and every error says what to change. We would rather show a failure than hide it.

  3. 03

    Small surface, few surprises

    We add a feature when it is finished, not when it is announced. Pages are never stubs.

  4. 04

    Respect the recipient

    Unsubscribing is one click and final, and consent is required. Complaints affect your account rating.
