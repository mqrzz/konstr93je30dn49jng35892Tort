Rays are delivery

Unevenness is a living letter

The logo

The main logo is the sign and the word “geserd” in lowercase. In running text we write “Geserd” with a capital letter.

The sign alone: for avatars, app icons and places where the word is already nearby.

How to use it

Colour

Only light on dark or dark on light, no in-between shades.

Clear space

Leave free space of at least half the height of the sign on every side.

Minimum size

The sign from 16 pixels. The logo with the word from 96 pixels wide.

Do not change it

Do not alter ray lengths or angles, add rings, shadows or gradients, or stretch the sign.
