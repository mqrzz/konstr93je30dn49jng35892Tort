Security at geserd

How we protect your keys, your domains and your mail, what you are responsible for, and what we are still building.

Secrets

Secrets are never stored in the clear

API keys are shown to you once, at the moment you create them, and stored only as a one-way hash. Even we cannot read a key back: if you lose it, you create a new one and revoke the old one. The DKIM private key of every domain is encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM, using a master key that is kept outside the database.

  • Revoke any key instantly
  • Keys carry a prefix, so they are easy to spot in logs
  • Every request is logged with the key that made it

Sending

Mail is sent responsibly

Every message is signed with DKIM and checked against your domain's SPF before it leaves. New accounts start in a sandbox that only allows sending to their own verified address, and every plan has daily and monthly limits. Addresses that bounce or complain go on a suppression list automatically, so a bad list cannot quietly damage your reputation, or the reputation of everyone who shares our infrastructure.

  • Sandbox until a domain is verified
  • Daily and monthly limits on every plan
  • Automatic suppression of bounces and complaints

Data

Your data, under your control

We keep message content and logs only for the retention period of your plan, then delete them. You can delete a domain or your account at any time and its data goes with it. We do not sell your data, we do not read your messages for advertising, and access to stored mail is limited to operating and protecting the service.

  • Retention from 1 to 30 days, depending on the plan
  • Deleting an account deletes its data
  • No advertising and no sale of data

Protections at a glance

Six things that are on by default.

Encrypted signing keys

DKIM private keys are encrypted at rest and only decrypted in memory when a message is signed.

Hashed API keys

Keys are stored as one-way hashes and shown once. A leaked database does not leak working keys.

Encrypted connections

The API and dashboard are served over HTTPS. Outgoing mail uses TLS whenever the receiving server supports it.

Abuse prevention

Sandbox for new accounts, rate limits and suppression lists keep spam out and deliverability high.

Limited retention

Message content and logs are deleted after the retention period of your plan.

Audit trail

Requests and delivery events are logged so you can see what happened and when.

Shared responsibility

Security works when both sides do their part.

What we do

  • Operate and update the servers
  • Encrypt secrets at rest
  • Monitor delivery and abuse
  • Tell you promptly about problems that affect you

What you do

  • Keep API keys out of public code
  • Use one key per app and revoke unused ones
  • Send only to people who expect your mail
  • Keep your DNS provider account secure

Security questions

Where is my data stored?

On servers we operate ourselves. Your messages are not passed to third-party analytics or advertising services.

Do you have security certifications?

Not yet, and we will not claim any certification before we actually hold it.

Can you read my emails?

Message content is stored so it can appear in your dashboard and logs. Access to it is limited to running and protecting the service.

What if my API key leaks?

Revoke it in the dashboard and create a new one. Revoked keys stop working immediately.

How do I report a vulnerability?

Write to us through the contact page with as much detail as you can. We read every report and respond as soon as we can.
