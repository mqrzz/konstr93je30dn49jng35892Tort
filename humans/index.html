What runs by itself

Eight things nobody does by hand

  1. 01

    Domain checks

    DNS records are checked again and again until they are right, and you are told what is missing.

  2. 02

    Delivery queue

    Messages are signed, queued, retried and logged without anyone watching.

  3. 03

    Bounces and complaints

    Addresses that bounce or complain are put on your suppression list on their own.

  4. 04

    Spam scoring

    Incoming mail is scored, and the result is stored with the message.

  5. 05

    Account rating

    A score from 0 to 100 reflects how your sending behaves, calculated by rules, not by opinion.

  6. 06

    Abuse detection

    Signs of duplicate accounts are detected automatically and a proportionate step is chosen.

  7. 07

    Status and notices

    The status page, limit warnings and maintenance notices go out without anyone pressing a button.

  8. 08

    Billing

    Payments, renewals and expiry are handled by the system and written to your notifications.
