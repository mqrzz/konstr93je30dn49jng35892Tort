Changelog

What changed in Geserd, newest first.

Metrics and Logs

  • Metrics now run on real data: sent, delivered, bounced, complained, delayed and received per hour or day, with rates and a comparison to the previous period
  • Delivery health shows your bounce and complaint rates against the recommended targets, plus the top recipient domains and the reasons for bounces
  • Metric history is kept longer than logs: 7, 30, 90 or 365 days depending on your plan. Export any range to CSV
  • Logs: every API request made with your keys and every SMTP submission, with filters, search, full request and response, and CSV export. Secrets are hidden and large bodies are shortened
  • A second Events tab lists every delivery event per message, including the server response for bounces
  • Broadcast pages now show results: delivered, bounced, spam complaints and unsubscribed, deliveries by hour, and the addresses behind each. Results are saved, so they stay after the logs expire
  • Support tickets are now a conversation: full-screen chat on phones, a compact message box, a confirmation before closing, and closed tickets are final (create a new ticket for a new question)
  • Help page redesigned with icons; the status link opens in a new tab, and feedback is now a ticket topic instead of a link to Contacts
  • Email previews have a light and dark mode switch and no longer show a blank white block in the dark theme; the progress label on broadcasts is no longer squashed
  • Delivery health now shows a 0 to 100 score where higher is better, next to the bounce and complaint rates

Broadcasts and audiences

  • Audiences: contact lists with CSV import and export, search and per-contact unsubscribe
  • Broadcasts: write once, preview, send a test, send now or schedule
  • Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link and header, and a public unsubscribe page
  • If your plan limit is reached, a broadcast pauses and continues by itself when the limit resets

Region checks, account region and Robokassa billing

  • Country check now runs on the Geserd server for pages and the API, and the unavailable page checks your IP itself
  • Supported countries page lists available and unavailable countries
  • Browser tab titles follow the selected language
  • New sign-up step: name and a required account region
  • Billing: plans paid in rubles through Robokassa, auto-renew, invoices and a clear payment history

Authentication, service settings and a better editor

  • Authentication module: send and check email one-time codes with two API calls
  • Service settings: sender defaults, spam protection level, suppression switches, allowed IP addresses
  • Account settings moved to their own page with sign-in methods and a data export
  • New mobile top bar and menu, a real settings icon, calmer notifications
  • Templates: search, sorting, undo and redo, drag and drop, list, quote and links blocks, preview text, version history, usage counts
  • Notifications: filters and delete read

Templates and the email editor

  • Templates and a visual email editor with live preview and variables
  • Send with template_id and variables through the API
  • Send a test email from the editor
  • API reference brought up to date

SMTP, spam score, support and status

  • SMTP sending on ports 465 and 587 with an API key as the password
  • Spam score for every outgoing email and a check endpoint before sending
  • Support tickets inside the dashboard and a live status page with 90-day history
  • Notifications for bounces, complaints, limits, domains and support replies
  • Account settings, sessions and per-type email alerts
