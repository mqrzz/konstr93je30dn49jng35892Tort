Changelog

What changed in Geserd, newest first.

Search and filters on lists, a preview text for broadcasts, and a calmer mobile cabinet

  • API keys, broadcasts and webhooks now have search and a filter
  • Broadcasts have a preview text that shows next to the subject in the inbox
  • Lists of audiences, keys, broadcasts, webhooks, templates and notifications are shorter and easier to read on a phone
  • Editor headers on a phone keep the main button in view and move the rest into a menu
  • Variables look the same everywhere: {{{name}}}

Segments, properties and topics for audiences, restricted API keys and email search

  • Audiences now have Properties, Segments and Topics tabs: group contacts, store your own fields such as company, and let people choose what they receive
  • Send a broadcast to a segment or a topic, and use your own contact properties as variables in the message
  • The unsubscribe page lets people turn individual topics on or off instead of leaving the whole list
  • API keys can be limited to sending only and to one domain
  • You can search sent and received emails, and delete buttons are now clearly red

A more consistent dashboard, clearer notifications and a tidier documentation header on phones

  • Buttons, fields, cards and dialogs in the dashboard now share one set of sizes and corners, in both light and dark themes
  • Notifications are rounder and the same width, with clear icons for success, error and information
  • The Onboarding, Integrations, Documents and Labs pages in settings now appear in your language
  • On phones the documentation header keeps the menu, Ask AI and Get started in one row, and long page titles wrap instead of being cut
  • Fixed text being cut off on several public pages on small screens

Two-factor sign-in, company SSO, branded emails and a bigger documentation

  • Two-factor authentication with an authenticator app: turn it on in account settings, scan a QR code and keep eight recovery codes
  • Single sign-on for companies is connected by our team under the Enterprise plan, with a new handbook article that explains how it works
  • Every email from Geserd now has the same branded design, in your language, and planned maintenance can be announced by email and in the dashboard
  • The documentation grew by fourteen pages: core concepts, a going-live checklist, guides for Node.js, Python and PHP, receiving and replying, and separate API pages for emails, domains, keys, webhooks and templates
  • A new Deliverability category in the Handbook with articles on why mail lands in spam, bounces and limits, how we fight spam and single sign-on
  • New interactive sections: a volume calculator on the pricing page, a live DNS check, a webhook event stream with signature check, and a savings view for the Siberia tariff

Handbook, blog and docs redone

  • The Handbook now has its own home page with a search (Ctrl+K) and 14 full articles in four languages: company, engineering, support and design
  • The blog uses the same article layout, with a side menu and a card with reading time
  • The docs section for beginners is now called “I’m not a developer” and has seven guides, including adding DNS records step by step and what to do if an email does not arrive; sections in the side menu open and close
  • The home page has a new animated section showing how a message travels there and back, and a row of Handbook covers
  • The docs on a phone got a cleaner top bar, and the gap before the “Was this page helpful?” buttons is gone
  • New animated sections on the maintenance, blocked account, security and contact pages; the quickstart no longer mentions a sandbox, because sending from an unverified domain is simply refused

Handbook, beginner docs and a new footer

  • New Handbook with eight short articles on how Geserd is run: why we exist, what we believe, how we ship changes, handle incidents, protect data and help you
  • New Beginners section in the docs: what SMTP is, what DNS records do, how DKIM, SPF and DMARC protect your name, and your first email step by step
  • The About page was rewritten from scratch, the footer now has five columns and a live status pill, and the top bar stays transparent until you scroll
  • Maintenance mode: we can pause sending or the whole service for planned work and show a clear notice instead of errors
  • Fixed: browser tab titles of the new pages follow your language, the next-article link in the Handbook is translated, and article headings no longer repeat

Legal documents and account safety

  • All legal documents were rewritten in full: Terms of Service, Privacy Policy (with sections for Russia and Europe), Cookie Policy, Acceptable Use, Payment Terms, Refund Policy, Data Processing Agreement, Eligibility and Sanctions Policy and a subprocessors page, in English and Russian
  • Accounts under review can be paused: sending stops, the dashboard stays available and you are told how to reach support

Account rating and the Siberia tariff

  • New Profile page with an account rating from 0 to 100 (100 is the best), built from your delivery quality, your own domain, account age, broadcast conduct and a complete profile, with tips on how to raise it
  • Siberia tariff for businesses and services that work in Siberia: three times the free limits and 50% off Pro and Business, with its own page, a pricing page banner and an application form in your account
  • Applications check the tax ID (INN) checksum and are reviewed by the Geserd team; the decision arrives as a notification

Metrics and Logs

  • Metrics now run on real data: sent, delivered, bounced, complained, delayed and received per hour or day, with rates and a comparison to the previous period
  • Delivery health shows your bounce and complaint rates against the recommended targets, plus the top recipient domains and the reasons for bounces
  • Metric history is kept longer than logs: 7, 30, 90 or 365 days depending on your plan. Export any range to CSV
  • Logs: every API request made with your keys and every SMTP submission, with filters, search, full request and response, and CSV export. Secrets are hidden and large bodies are shortened
  • A second Events tab lists every delivery event per message, including the server response for bounces
  • Broadcast pages now show results: delivered, bounced, spam complaints and unsubscribed, deliveries by hour, and the addresses behind each. Results are saved, so they stay after the logs expire
  • Support tickets are now a conversation: full-screen chat on phones, a compact message box, a confirmation before closing, and closed tickets are final (create a new ticket for a new question)
  • Help page redesigned with icons; the status link opens in a new tab, and feedback is now a ticket topic instead of a link to Contacts
  • Email previews have a light and dark mode switch and no longer show a blank white block in the dark theme; the progress label on broadcasts is no longer squashed
  • Delivery health now shows a 0 to 100 score where higher is better, next to the bounce and complaint rates

Broadcasts and audiences

  • Audiences: contact lists with CSV import and export, search and per-contact unsubscribe
  • Broadcasts: write once, preview, send a test, send now or schedule
  • Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link and header, and a public unsubscribe page
  • If your plan limit is reached, a broadcast pauses and continues by itself when the limit resets

Region checks, account region and Robokassa billing

  • Country check now runs on the Geserd server for pages and the API, and the unavailable page checks your IP itself
  • Supported countries page lists available and unavailable countries
  • Browser tab titles follow the selected language
  • New sign-up step: name and a required account region
  • Billing: plans paid in rubles through Robokassa, auto-renew, invoices and a clear payment history

Authentication, service settings and a better editor

  • Authentication module: send and check email one-time codes with two API calls
  • Service settings: sender defaults, spam protection level, suppression switches, allowed IP addresses
  • Account settings moved to their own page with sign-in methods and a data export
  • New mobile top bar and menu, a real settings icon, calmer notifications
  • Templates: search, sorting, undo and redo, drag and drop, list, quote and links blocks, preview text, version history, usage counts
  • Notifications: filters and delete read

Templates and the email editor

  • Templates and a visual email editor with live preview and variables
  • Send with template_id and variables through the API
  • Send a test email from the editor
  • API reference brought up to date

SMTP, spam score, support and status

  • SMTP sending on ports 465 and 587 with an API key as the password
  • Spam score for every outgoing email and a check endpoint before sending
  • Support tickets inside the dashboard and a live status page with 90-day history
  • Notifications for bounces, complaints, limits, domains and support replies
  • Account settings, sessions and per-type email alerts
