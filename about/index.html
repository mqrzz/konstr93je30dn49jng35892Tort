Why we built it

The same setup, every time

Almost every project ends up rebuilding the same email plumbing, usually after something has already gone wrong in production.

DNS first, reputation later

Records, DKIM, SPF, and a sender nobody trusts yet. Every project starts by rebuilding this.

geserd._domainkey TXT
v=DKIM1; k=rsa; p=MIIBIjAN…
@ TXT v=spf1 include:…
_dmarc TXT v=DMARC1; p=none

Mail fails quietly

Without events you only find out about a bounce from an unhappy customer.

delivered anna@gmail.com
bounced  nobody@yahoo.com
delayed   sam@mail.ru
complained kim@gmail.com

Replies need a home

Receiving means MX records, parsing and storage, so most teams skip it and lose the conversation.

{
  "from": "ada@example.com",
  "subject": "Re: Welcome",
  "text": "Thanks, it works!"
}

So it all sits behind one API

Sending and receiving, both directions, with logs and webhooks included from day one.

POST /api/emails
GET  /api/emails/{id}
POST your-webhook
HTTP 200

One clear way beats five configurable ones. We say what works today and what does not. We keep our limits even when they are inconvenient, because deliverability is a promise to every sender.
